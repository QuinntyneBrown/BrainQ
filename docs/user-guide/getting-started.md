# 1. Getting Started

BrainQ is a single-owner personal brain: every Note, Idea, Person, Project, and Commitment you capture lives in one connected graph. There's no inbox, no folders, and no shared workspace. Everything you put in is yours, and everything you see is derived from what you've put in.

## What's on screen

When BrainQ loads, you land on the **Today** screen. The window splits into three regions:

| Region | xs / md | xl |
|---|---|---|
| **Stage** | Full-width content for the active screen | Centred content column |
| **Tab bar** | Pinned to the bottom — Today, Brain, Capture (centre disc), Search | Hidden |
| **Side rail** | Hidden | Pinned to the left — Today, Brain, Search, plus a Capture entry and a footer storage line |
| **Context pane** | Hidden | Pinned to the right — varies per screen (graph for Detail, leaderboard for Brain, etc.) |

A floating **cog button** sits in the bottom-right of every screen — that's the [Tweaks](personalization.md) panel.

## The five entity types

Everything you capture is one of these five types. Type colours the icon (its "glyph"), and unlocks a type-specific stats card on the [Detail](detail.md) screen.

| Type | Glyph | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Note** | notepad (page with folded corner) | Anything plain — a thought, a quote, a passing observation |
| **Idea** | lightbulb | Something you might do, build, or explore |
| **Person** | head + shoulders silhouette | A real person — gets last-seen, touchpoints, and the RecallQ surface |
| **Project** | clipboard (rectangle with two horizontal lines) | A unit of work with a status, due date, and progress |
| **Commitment** | clock circle (circle with one minute hand) | A recurring practice — gets a streak, a heatmap, and a daily Log button |

## The capture-first habit

Most of your time in BrainQ is reading and connecting; most of your *value* in BrainQ is captured in the first 30 seconds of a thought, before it fades. Get into the muscle of tapping **Capture** the moment something is on your mind. The system handles type inference, suggested links, and connections after the fact.
